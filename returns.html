Returns & refunds

What to do if your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, and how replacements or refunds are handled.

01Policy summary

For research integrity reasons, we do not accept change-of-mind returns. If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos so we can review it quickly.

02Non-returnable items

For safety, product integrity, and regulatory reasons, the following are not eligible for return:

  • Opened products (broken seal, removed cap, removed crimp).
  • Used or partially used products.
  • Items with broken tamper-evident integrity.
  • Change-of-mind returns.
  • Items returned without prior approval from support.
This policy exists to protect the integrity of every product in the supply chain. We cannot accept returns of opened research compounds under any circumstances.

03Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged in transit, or you receive the wrong product, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with:

  • Your order number.
  • Clear photos of the outer parcel, inner packaging, and the product label.
  • A brief description of the issue.

We will review and resolve the issue quickly — typically by sending a replacement at no additional cost, or issuing a full refund where a replacement is not possible.

04Refund method

Approved refunds are issued back through the original payment route where supported, or by another agreed refund route where appropriate. Processing times depend on the payment provider or bank.

You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been issued.

05Return shipping

Where the issue is our error or the parcel arrives damaged, UKMAXX will advise the next step. If a return is required, we will provide instructions and cover reasonable return shipping costs. Do not send anything back without support approval.

06Process & timing

Once your issue is approved by support:

  1. You will receive a support reference and clear next steps.
  2. If a return is required, we will provide the return instructions before anything is sent back.
  3. Where a replacement is appropriate, we will arrange it as quickly as possible.
  4. Where a refund is approved, we will issue it promptly and confirm by email.

Questions? Contact our support team with your order number and we'll help.
