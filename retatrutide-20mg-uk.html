What do RT20, RT 20mg and Reta 20mg mean?

RT20 is the UKMAXX product code for the Retatrutide 20mg single-vial listing. RT 20mg, RT20mg and Reta 20mg are shorthand for the same stated strength; they are not separate UKMAXX compounds or batches. Current pricing and availability remain on the RT20 product page.

Retatrutide 20mg assay and purity result

For batch RT20-2026-08-A, Janoshik Analytical reported 23.20mg measured Retatrutide content in the tested sample and 99.607% purity. Report #225850 records a Common GLP-1 peptide blind test analysed by HPLC.

Assay and purity answer different questions. The assay reports the measured amount of target material in the analysed sample. Purity describes the proportion of detected material represented by the target compound. Publishing both gives a fuller view than presenting a purity percentage alone.
