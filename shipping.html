01Where we currently deliver

Online checkout currently accepts United Kingdom delivery addresses only. If checkout does not accept your address, contact us before attempting payment.

Outside the UK? Use the international enquiry button above to ask whether delivery to your country is currently available. An enquiry does not guarantee availability.

02Price & delivery window

Standard UK delivery costs £4.99 and is free when the discounted basket value reaches £100. Most UK shipments arrive within 1–2 working days after dispatch via Royal Mail Tracked 24.

Tracked 24 is a delivery aim rather than a guaranteed next-day service. Delays may occur during peak periods, weather events or Royal Mail disruption.

03Dispatch times

Orders placed before 2PM (UK time) are typically dispatched the same working day. Orders placed after 2PM, or over a weekend or public holiday, dispatch on the next working day.

Cut-off: 14:00 UK time, Mon–Fri, excluding UK public holidays. Orders after cut-off are treated as next-working-day orders.

04Tracking

Tracking information is emailed once the carrier has scanned the parcel into its network—usually the same evening as dispatch or the next working morning. You can follow the shipment on Royal Mail or through the UKMAXX tracking page.

05Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for ensuring the shipping address provided at checkout is accurate and complete. UKMAXX is not responsible for delays or losses caused by incorrect or incomplete addresses. If you spot an address error, contact support within 1 hour of placing the order and we will attempt to amend it before dispatch.

06Failed delivery & return to sender

If a parcel cannot be delivered and is returned to sender, we will contact you to arrange redelivery. Re-shipping fees apply to cover the second delivery. We will not re-ship until the re-shipping fee is paid.

07Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel is delayed beyond 5 UK working days after dispatch, or arrives damaged, contact support with your order number. We will open a trace with the carrier and, where appropriate, send a replacement or issue a refund. We may ask for a photo of the damaged parcel and contents.
