Why UKMAXX

Transparency from test to dispatch.

Every released compound batch follows a documented standard: independent analysis, public reporting and batch-level traceability.

Independent analysis

Each released compound batch is analysed by an independent third-party laboratory. Identity, purity and/or assay result, method and batch details are documented in its published COA.

Published without exceptions

Passed batches are published before release, while rejected batches remain visible in the public record. Results are not hidden when a batch fails UKMAXX release specifications.

Batch-level traceability

Batch codes and QR-linked records connect each supplied compound to its published report. When a new batch replaces the current one, it receives its own test record and batch identity.
