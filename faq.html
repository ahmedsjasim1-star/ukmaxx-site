Orders & Payments 5

How quickly do you dispatch?

Orders placed before 2PM (UK time) are usually dispatched same working day. Orders after 2PM typically dispatch the next working day. You will receive a dispatch confirmation with tracking by email.

What payment methods do you accept?

Checkout is processed securely via Pay by Bank. At checkout you choose your bank, approve the bank-to-bank payment in your banking app or online banking, then return to UKMAXX once the payment is confirmed. No card details are needed. Read the Pay by Bank guide for the full flow.

What should I do if Pay by Bank does not complete?

Your order is confirmed only after the payment succeeds and you receive confirmation from UKMAXX. If your bank declines, rejects or leaves the payment incomplete, you can return to checkout and try again. If the problem continues, contact support with the order reference shown on screen. Never send a separate bank transfer unless UKMAXX support has confirmed the correct order details.

How does the MAXX10 discount work?

MAXX10 applies to eligible full-price products and bundles. In a mixed basket, the saving is calculated only against eligible items, and checkout shows which products received the discount. Enter the code before starting payment and check the displayed order total.

Can I change or cancel an order?

If your order has not yet been dispatched, contact support as soon as possible with your order reference and requested change. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot usually be amended.

Shipping & Delivery 5

Do you ship from the UK?

Yes. All stock is held in the UK and we currently ship to UK addresses only.

Can customers outside the UK order?

Online checkout is currently UK-only. If you are outside the UK, you can contact UKMAXX on WhatsApp to ask whether your location can be supported. An enquiry does not guarantee availability or delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Most UK deliveries arrive in 1–2 working days after dispatch via Royal Mail Tracked 24. Times may vary during peak periods or due to Royal Mail disruption.

Is shipping discreet?

Yes. All parcels are packed in plain, unbranded outer packaging for standard courier handling. The contents are not described on the outside of the parcel.

How do I track my order?

Open the UKMAXX order tracker and enter your order reference and checkout email. Once Royal Mail tracking is available, the tracking page provides a direct link to the carrier record.

Batch Verification & COA 5

Are batches independently tested?

Released UKMAXX peptide and coenzyme batches are submitted to a named third-party laboratory and linked to their published analytical records. The exact method and scope vary by product and are shown on the corresponding batch record. Support items such as BAC Water may use a different quality-control framework and are labelled accordingly.

How do I verify my batch?

Use the batch code on your verification card and compare it against the UKMAXX COA checker. The checker shows active, archived and rejected batch records, including sample, method, date, purity, lab and assay details. If anything does not match, contact support immediately.

Can I verify the laboratory report directly?

Where the laboratory provides public verification, UKMAXX links to the original laboratory-hosted record as well as displaying the relevant batch details. Start with the COA checker and use the report link shown for that batch.

Does a COA prove the contents of every vial?

No. A laboratory report documents the sample submitted for analysis. Batch codes, batch boundaries, sample photography and consistent records help connect that result to released stock, but a single analytical test is not an individual test of every vial. Read the research peptide verification checklist for the full evidence chain.

What if I cannot find my batch code?

Contact support with a clear photo of the label and your order details. The team can run a traceability check and confirm the batch against our records.

Stock & Reviews 3

What happens when a product is sold out?

Ordering is disabled when the live inventory reaches zero. The sold-through batch record remains available for traceability, and the product page offers a restock notification where applicable. A new batch is not released until its required checks and documentation are complete.

How do batch and restock alerts work?

Enter your email through a batch-alert or restock form and select the updates you want. UKMAXX uses that address for the requested product, COA or batch-release updates. Every marketing update includes an unsubscribe option.

Can I review more than one product from an order?

Yes. Eligible delivered orders can submit a separate review for each distinct product purchased. After the first submission, the review page shows any remaining products from the order that are still available to review.

Product Handling 3

How should products be stored?

Follow the storage information supplied for the specific product and the validated protocol used by your laboratory. Storage requirements can vary by compound, formulation and research workflow. UKMAXX does not provide instructions for human preparation or use.

Do you provide usage or dosing advice?

No. UKMAXX does not provide medical, human-use, or dosing advice of any kind. All products are supplied strictly for laboratory and in-vitro research use only.

Can I return products?

We do not accept change-of-mind returns for research integrity reasons. If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact support within 48 hours of delivery. See the full Returns policy for details.

No. All products are sold strictly for laboratory and in-vitro research purposes only. They are not intended for human or animal use, and are not medicines, food supplements, or cosmetics.
