Common questions

What is Pay by Bank?

Pay by Bank is a secure Open Banking payment. At checkout you choose your bank, approve the payment in your banking app or online banking, and return to UKMAXX once it is confirmed. UKMAXX never sees or stores your bank login details or card details.

Is this product tested before dispatch?

Yes. Every UKMAXX batch is independently tested by a third-party laboratory (typically Janoshik Analytical) before it's released for sale. The COA certificate is accessible via the batch verification card included with each order.

How is the product shipped?

All orders ship from UK stock via Royal Mail Tracked 24. Orders placed before 2PM (UK time) on a working day are dispatched the same day in discreet, tamper-evident packaging. Tracking is sent by email once dispatched.

What is the storage requirement?

Store sealed vials refrigerated at 2–8°C, dry and protected from light. After reconstitution, keep refrigerated and follow the batch insert guidance. Avoid heat, light and repeated temperature cycling.

Can I return the product?

For research integrity reasons, we do not accept change-of-mind returns. If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact support within 48 hours of delivery and we'll review it quickly.
