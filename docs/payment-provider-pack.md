## 6. What we need from the payment provider

We need a provider that can explicitly support this category after review, not a generic low-risk account that may be closed later.

Ideal options:

1. Card-processing merchant account and payment gateway suitable for research-use peptide/lab supply ecommerce.
2. Pay-by-bank/open-banking checkout as a faster fallback or secondary payment method.
3. Clear underwriting requirements before integration.
4. Confirmation of supported product category in writing.
5. Settlement to Tide or guidance if a different business bank is required.

## 7. Questions to ask on the call

- Can you support UK ecommerce sales of research-use peptide compounds and laboratory reconstitution supplies?
- Do your acquiring/payment partners explicitly allow this category after underwriting?
- Would you classify this as pharmacy, supplements, research chemicals, lab supplies, or another category?
- Do you support card payments, pay-by-bank/open-banking, or both?
- Can settlement go to a Tide business account?
- What documents do you need from us?
- What are the expected approval timelines?
- What are the setup fees, monthly fees, transaction fees, rolling reserve and settlement times?
- Is there a minimum processing volume?
- Do you require changes to website copy, terms, policies, product pages, age gate or checkout?
- Do you provide hosted checkout, API integration, payment links, or WooCommerce-style plugins?
- How are refunds, chargebacks and disputes handled?
- Can you provide written confirmation that this business/category is approved before we switch live traffic?
