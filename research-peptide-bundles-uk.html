Bundle FAQ

Before you build.

Is Build Your Own a fixed-price product?

No. Its price is calculated from the three selected single vials, followed by the automatic 5% bundle saving. The basket shows the calculation before checkout.

Can the same product be selected three times?

Yes, provided sufficient live stock is available. The builder prevents selection beyond current availability.

Does MAXX10 work on research bundles?

Yes. Current fixed bundles and the Build Your Own bundle are eligible. The basket displays the saving before payment starts.

Does one COA cover a mixed bundle?

No. Different compounds retain separate batch records. A same-product three-pack points to the listed batch for that product, while a mixed selection can involve several records.
